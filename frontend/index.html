Word Association Game

Test your divergent thinking

You will be shown a prompt word. Respond with a word that is related to the prompt, but try to take the chain in a new, creative direction each time.

Your response becomes the next prompt. Over 20 turns, build a chain of connected yet divergent associations.

Words must be related to the prompt (no random words!) the challenge is finding creative connections, not obvious ones.
