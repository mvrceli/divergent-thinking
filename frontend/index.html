Word Association Game

Test your divergent thinking

How to play

You will be given a starting word. Type a word that you associate with it: your word then becomes the new prompt. Keep going for 20 turns, building a chain of associations.

At some point during the game, a suggestion will appear alongside your current prompt. When that happens, try to enter a word that connects both words together. After a few turns the suggestion will disappear and the game continues as normal.

Please be patient — the game may take a moment to load suggestions. Do not refresh the page.

There are no right or wrong answers. You cannot fail this game.
